# Earthborne Rangers Fan-Made

Unofficial fan-made adventures for [Earthborne Rangers](https://earthbornegames.com/), by Filippo "Pence".

**Play them here: https://filippopence.github.io/earthborne-rangers-fan-made/**

Each adventure comes with an interactive campaign guide to follow entry by entry, the cards it adds, and print-and-play files.

## Adventures

- **Peril in Ancestor's Grove**: a stand-alone, three-day adventure in the style of the One-Day Missions. A wounded buck near an ancient ruin hints at a malady that threatens the balance of the forest.
- **Missing Supplies**: a stand-alone investigation for 1 to 24 players in up to 6 groups. Supplies are disappearing from Ranger caches across the Valley: visit the caches, gather clues and work out who is responsible.

You need a copy of Earthborne Rangers to play.
