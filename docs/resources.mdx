Extra material that is handy at the table, whichever adventure you play.

## Campaign Log card

A card-sized campaign log for short adventures: three day boxes to check off, plus room to note the missions you hold and the notable events you record. It was made for "Peril in Ancestor's Grove", which lasts three days, but works for any short story.
