## Printing the cards

- Print at **100% / actual size** (disable "fit to page"), otherwise the cards will not match sleeve size.
- Print duplex and **flip on the long edge**. Alternatively print both pages single-sided, cut, and sleeve the two faces back to back.
- Cut on the marks at the edges of the sheet: there are two cuts between neighbouring cards. The artwork continues about 1.5 mm past each cut (3 mm on the outside of the sheet), so a slightly off cut does not leave a white edge.

## You also need

A copy of Earthborne Rangers and its Lure of the Valley campaign guide: this adventure only replaces a few entries and uses cards from the core set.
