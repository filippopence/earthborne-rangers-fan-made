> The buck’s eyes plead for help, and you feel a surge of determination. Gently, you reach into your backpack, extracting a basic first aid kit. The wound, however, is unlike any you have encountered before, and the effects of a grievous infection are evident.
>
> You must do your best in finding herbs that grow nearby and apply what you’ve learned during your training to make an antibiotic.

Search the Woods set for a Sitka Buck and put it into play along the way exhausted. Attach the **WOUNDED BUCK** mission to that being.

<Objective>Collect 2 different Food and clear the wounded Sitka Buck to give it the antibiotic.</Objective>
