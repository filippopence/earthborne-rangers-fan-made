title: "1.08 REMOVE THE THREAT"
---

> The cries and roars of several predators wake you up before dawn, you are at the reckoning: you have hunted the predator but now you are its prey!

Add the Leader of the Pack (recorded in the campaign log) along the way with 1 slime token on it. Then, based on the current day:

- **Day 2:** The Lead Ranger searches the collection for an additional copy of the Leader of the Pack and put it within reach.
- **Day 3:** The Lead Ranger searches the collection for each copy of the Leader of the Pack and put it within reach.
