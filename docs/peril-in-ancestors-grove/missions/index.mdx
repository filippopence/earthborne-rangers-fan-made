## Getting Started

First, perform the first 4 steps of setup on page 10 of the rulebook, then return here for the remainder of setup.

> The sun is just beginning to cast its golden rays across the dense forest as you make your way down the narrow trail. The morning dew glistens on the leaves, creating a serene atmosphere that belies the untamed nature of the wilderness. You walk briskly, your footsteps blending with the rustling of leaves under your sturdy boots.
>
> As you approach an ancient construction emerging from the heart of the forest, a peculiar sight catches your eye. There, near the ruin, stands a majestic buck, its antlers reaching proudly into the air. It seems to be frozen in place; its eyes fixed on something in the distance. Intrigued and concerned, you slow your pace.

Put the **ANCESTOR'S GROVE** location card and the **A PERFECT DAY** weather card into play in the surroundings.

> As you draw closer, the buck turns at you, showing his side and you notice a large, gaping wound. The fur around the injury is stained with dried white slime. It is a sight that speaks of more than a mere accident—it hints at a malady that threatens the delicate balance of the forest.
>
> The buck, sensing your presence, turns its head and locks eyes with you. There is a silent understanding between you — a shared acknowledgment of the pain and vulnerability that life in the wild often entails.
>
> The forest, once a sanctuary, now holds a sinister secret that demands unraveling. You know you must act swiftly.

#### Rangers choose:

<Choice>A. Care for the buck:</Choice>

Gain the **WOUNDED BUCK** mission. (Put its card into play in the surroundings.)

<Choice>B. Thwart the threat:</Choice>

Gain the **CHASE THE THREAT** mission. (Put its card into play in the surroundings.)

**» Continue Reading:**

Create the path deck by shuffling together the Woods set and 3 random cards from The Valley set (see page 15 of the rulebook). Place it above the surroundings.

Then, complete setup by performing the setup steps on the back of the location card and placing tokens on all cards that need them.
