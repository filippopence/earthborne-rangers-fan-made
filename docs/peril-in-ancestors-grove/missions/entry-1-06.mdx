title: "1.06 CHASE THE THREAT"
---

> You decided to investigate the nature of the threat. It should be a predator with a lot of weird slime coming out of its mouth, and you are going to track it through the forest.

Build the predator deck by shuffling together one copy of Skittish Opilion, Romping Lutrinal, Prowling Wolhund, Bloodbeckoned Velox and Hungry Scrof respectively from the Ravine, Lakeshore, Woods and Grasslands sets. Then pick one without looking at it and set it facedown aside, out of play.

<Objective>Travel among locations in the Valley, scout the predator's deck and identify it before the end of the day.</Objective>
