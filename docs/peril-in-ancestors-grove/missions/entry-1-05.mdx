> You prepare a vegetable paste with ripe fruits and globular flowers with a sweet and sugary scent, those that Hy believes are excellent for quickly recharging energy, and after disinfecting the wound, you spread some on the flaps of skin around the buck’s wound. Its laboured breathing eases, and a spark of vitality returns to its eyes. After some minutes the buck rises to its feet and looks firmly into your eyes. It hesitates for a moment, as if expressing gratitude, before bounding away into the depths of the woods.
