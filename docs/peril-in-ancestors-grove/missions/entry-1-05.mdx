> You prepare a vegetable paste with ripe fruits and globular flowers with a sweet and sugary scent, those that Hy believes are excellent for quickly recharging energy, and after disinfecting the wound, you spread some on the skin flaps of the stag. The buck’s laboured breathing eases, and a spark of vitality returns to its eyes. After some minutes the buck stands up on his feet and it firmly looks into your eyes. It hesitates for a moment, as if expressing gratitude, before bounding away into the depths of the woods.
