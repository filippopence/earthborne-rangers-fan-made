> Lunn turns at you: “I found something at Boulder Field that I can’t stop thinking about,” he says.
>
> He carefully withdraws a lantern from his pack: glass, ornate, containing a soft yellowish glow suspended in clear fluid. “The craftsmanship is extraordinary. I’ve seen similar devices in the Arcology, but this one feels different. Personal. Well-loved.”
>
> He examines it thoughtfully, turning it gently in his hands. “Someone treasured this. But it was simply left there, abandoned near the cache.” He looks at you, puzzled. “Who forgets something like this? It troubles me, not in a mysterious way, but in a practical one. This is too fine to abandon by accident.”
