> Hy looks up as you approach. “Ah, good timing,” he says, his expression growing serious as you brief him on the state of the cache you found and restocked.
>
> He shakes his head, clearly troubled. “Such waste,” he mutters, almost to himself. “Good provisions, simply left to rot or decay. It pains me to see resources squandered like this.”
>
> After a moment, he composes himself and pulls out a small pouch from his pack. “But I’ve discovered something at the cache near Crossroads Station. Crumbs of cream and chocolate mixed together. It’s not a recipe I usually prepare myself, but it’s a very common snack throughout the Valley, and it’s the favourite snack of many inhabitants.”
