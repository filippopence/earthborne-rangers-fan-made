---
title: "End of the Day"
---

Your day in the Valley has come to an end, but your investigation is not over. When you’re ready to continue, you can return to the Valley.

When you resume play, you will begin at the location where your day ended, using the same terrain set you were exploring. Put the A Perfect Day weather card into play in the surroundings, and set up the location as normal.

Additionally, put into play the final mission card you received before your day ended, maintaining all progress you have noted (checked off bubbles and clues) on your campaign tracker.
