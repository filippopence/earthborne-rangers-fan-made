> You find Ben Amon near the river’s edge, checking the rigging on one of the hoversled-like craft used for swift travel through the Valley. He looks up as you approach, and his expression is all business. “Tumbledown,” he says. “I’m making a run down there. That’s far enough from here that fewer people travel that way, which means fewer eyes on our cache locations. If someone’s been methodical about this, they might think that distance offers them cover.”
>
> He tightens a final knot with practiced precision. “Check our caches in that region thoroughly. Look for patterns, the way things are disturbed, what’s been taken, what’s been left behind. Swift travel means I can cover a lot of ground quickly, but I need Rangers on the ground doing the detailed work.”
>
> He climbs into his craft and begins preparing for departure.
