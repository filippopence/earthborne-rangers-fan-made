**Boulder Field**

> As you finish restocking the cache near Boulder Field, something catches your eye. On a nearby rock outcropping, positioned as if to provide light while someone worked, you discover a strange apparatus. A lantern of sorts, fashioned from glass. Inside, suspended in what appears to be clear fluid, something glows with a soft, yellowish light. The fluid is in constant motion, swirling gently around whatever luminous thing dwells within, as if the lantern itself were somehow alive.
>
> You recognize the craftsmanship. You’ve seen similar devices in the Arcology: bioluminescent apparatus of some kind. But this one feels... different. Personal. Well-used. The kind of thing someone might carry with them on their travels, treasuring its gentle, living light. Whoever left it here simply forgot to retrieve it.
