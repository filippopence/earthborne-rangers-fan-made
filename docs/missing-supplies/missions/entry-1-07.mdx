> You find Lunn standing near one of the market stalls, engaged in lively conversation with a group of traders. When he sees you, his face lights up. “My friends! Perfect timing,” he says warmly, excusing himself from the conversation. “I’m heading over to Kobo’s Market. That place is always buzzing with activity and information, merchants see things, hear things, notice patterns. If something’s been moved through this Valley, someone at that market will know.”
>
> He gestures broadly at the bustling marketplace around him. “While I’m working the crowds, I need you Rangers to check our cache locations in this region. Search them carefully. Look for signs of tampering, anything that seems out of place. Between what I learn from the traders and what you find at those caches, we’ll start getting answers.”
>
> He winks at you. “Trust me, market folk talk if you know how to listen.” With that, he turns back toward the market with a spring in his step.
