**Philosopher’s Garden**

> Within the cache, you discover culinary ingredients scattered about: leaves, seeds, and roots that remind you of those Hy frequently uses in his recipes. They are arranged as if someone were cataloging or testing what provisions are available. Beside them lies a partially crossed-off list of ingredients, suggesting an ongoing inventory or experiment.

The Lead Ranger searches the Spire set for Hy Pimpot, Chef and puts them into play. Do not read their entry.

Check off the bubble matching your current location next to **UPPER EAST MAINTENANCE** on your campaign tracker.

If there are 3 bubbles checked, complete the **UPPER EAST MAINTENANCE** mission and gain **BACK TO SPIRE**.

<Objective>This Ranger Cache is now filled again. Travel to one of the other two locations to continue the mission.</Objective>
