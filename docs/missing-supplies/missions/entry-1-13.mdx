**Stoneweaver Bridge**

> As you finish restocking the cache, you notice Nal approaching along the trail. She moves with her characteristic fluid grace, and when she reaches you, there is a calm recognition in her eyes. “I felt drawn here by the spirits,” she says simply. “And now I understand why. You are here, doing what must be done. The Valley senses your efforts.” She pauses, then continues, her gaze turning distant once more. “I had a vision recently,” she says quietly. “It was unclear, as visions often are. Kal was there, I saw him moving with such speed and purpose through the Valley. But I could not discern whether he was in danger... or if he was the danger. The spirits were unclear on this matter.” She looks back at you, her brow slightly furrowed. “The energies around him were... conflicted. Ambitious. Restless. But nothing more than that. I hesitate to speak of it, for the images were too fragmented to trust.”
