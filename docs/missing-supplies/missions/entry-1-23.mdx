**Mount Nim**

> You find Calypsa at Mount Nim, finishing the work of restocking the cache. When she sees you, she lets out a long breath and offers a tired smile. “Glad you’re here,” she says, rolling their shoulders.
>
> “Last week... Quisi followed me everywhere. And I mean everywhere.” She shakes her head, not unkindly, but with clear exhaustion. “The girl is relentless. Questions, endless questions, running ahead, falling behind, pointing at things...” She pauses, actually laughing a little. “By comparison, traveling around the Valley refilling caches alone feels almost peaceful.”
>
> She looks out toward the horizon, her expression softening. “She means well, that one. But a little peace and quiet is a welcome change.”
