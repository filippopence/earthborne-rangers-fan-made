**The Plummet**

> You spot Lunn making his way through The Plummet, moving with the slow, deliberate steps of someone whose body is protesting every moment. When he sees you, his face brightens, though you notice the exhaustion etched into the lines around his eyes. “There you are!” he says warmly, though his voice carries the weight of weariness. “I’ve been thinking… I’m going to miss having Ben Amon’s Swift available. These past weeks, we’ve been heading in much the same directions. Elder Thrush has had him flying back and forth constantly, checking smoke clouds to the west, monitoring the first snowfall in the north, and it’s been lucky timing for me. Made getting around the Valley considerably easier.” He shifts his weight, wincing slightly. “These last few days with the market relocation and this mission... my back’s been paying the price for all the extra travel.”
