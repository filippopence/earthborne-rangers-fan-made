> As you make your way back to Spire, the weight of your investigation settles upon your shoulders. The town is quieter than before, as if holding its breath. You find your way to a small plaza near the market, where Elder Thrush stands waiting alongside several of the Rangers you encountered during your travels. Kordo, Nal, Hy, Calypsa, Ben Amon, and Lunn are all gathered here, their expressions a mix of hope and concern.
>
> When Elder Thrush spots you, she steps forward. “You’ve returned,” she says, her voice steady but expectant. “The Rangers here have shared what they’ve discovered, but the full picture requires all of us together. It’s time to piece together what’s behind these disappearances.”
>
> She gestures toward the assembled group. “Tell us what you’ve found. Every detail matters.”
